2. Surplus-risk flags (accuracy, precision, recall, F1, error rate)

Each morning a store holds its usual order: the same day last week plus 15%. The system flags a store-family line as at risk when its forecast says more than 10% of that stock will be left unsold, so the surplus can be listed for merchants early. 1,424 of 2,240 test days really had a surplus.

Precision: of the lines flagged, how many really had a surplus. Recall: of the lines that had a surplus, how many were flagged. F1 balances the two. "Same day last week" forecasts the same sales as last week, which is always below its own order plus 15%, so it flags every line: recall is 100% but precision is only the share of days that really had a surplus.
